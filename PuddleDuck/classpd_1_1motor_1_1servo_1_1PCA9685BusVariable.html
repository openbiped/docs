Detailed Description

template<uint16_t numServos, uint8_t defaultOEValue = true>
class pd::motor::servo::PCA9685BusVariable< numServos, defaultOEValue >

Implements Dispatch over i2c to PCA9685.

Implements Dispatch and spreads the PWM output to up to 62 PCA9685 modules (via i2c address 0x40-0x75). Theoretically allowing you to control up to 992 PWM outputs.

OE pin defaults to LOW.
